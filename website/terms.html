This website describes the HYDRAX system and lets you request a quote. It is not a store: there is no purchase flow, no payment processing and no price shown on this site, because pricing is prepared per farm after a quote request.

Requesting a quote

  • Submitting the request form does not create a contract, an order, or any obligation to buy.
  • A quote we send you in response is an estimate based on what you told us, not a binding offer, unless we say otherwise in writing at the time.
  • We may contact you using the details you provided to discuss your request.
  • Please give us accurate information — an inaccurate zone count or site description will produce an inaccurate quote.
