Who this policy covers

This policy covers the HYDRAX public website and the quote request form at /request. It does not cover the operator console (/admin), which is an internal tool for the team handling requests, or the monitoring dashboard, which displays farm telemetry rather than visitor data.

What we collect

When you submit the quote request form, we collect exactly the fields on that form:

  • Farm size and location, irrigation type, and number of zones
  • Which capabilities you are interested in
  • Your full name and phone number
  • Your email address, if you choose to give one — it is optional
  • Any free-text notes you add

Simply browsing the public website does not send us anything. There is no analytics script and no tracking pixel on this site — see Cookies and tracking below.

Submitting the form also causes your IP address to be checked against a rate limit, to stop the form being used to flood us with fake submissions. That check happens in server memory and is not written to the database or stored alongside your request.

Why we collect it

  • To review your farm's requirements and prepare a configuration and a quote.
  • To contact you about that quote, using the details you provided.
  • To improve how we prioritise features, using the capabilities you told us you want.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

Requests are stored in the HYDRAX backend's own database and are visible only to operators who authenticate to the admin console with the operator key. The public quote form itself cannot read back any customer's details — it can only create a new request and receive a reference number.

Cookies and tracking

The public website sets no cookies, loads no analytics, and makes no request to any third-party service. There is nothing here to consent to, and no cookie banner appears for that reason — adding one without a reason to would be its own kind of noise.

The operator console keeps its own sign-in key in the browser's sessionStorage for the operator's own tab, so it does not need to be re-entered on every reload. That storage is not a cookie, is never sent to any third party, and holds no data about site visitors or customers.

If analytics is added to this site in the future, this section will be updated first, and a consent mechanism will be added before any non-essential tracking is enabled.
